# JW Study Desk — assets

`logo.png` is the exact approved logo provided by the project owner, Clément Martin. `logo-monochrome.svg` and `tokens.json` come from the supplied **JW_Study_Desk_Identite_Visuelle.zip** brand kit. The monochrome SVG is scaled to Obsidian’s 100-unit icon coordinate system at registration; the full-color original is used in the interface.

`dawn-valley.webp` is a new panorama generated for this application on 2026-11-06. The original 2172 × 724 PNG was exported at the same resolution as WebP (quality 90). It is a decorative background; all live text and navigation remain native HTML.

Exact generation prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: panoramic header artwork for a professional Obsidian study application named JW Study Desk. Create ONLY a landscape illustration, NO interface, NO text, NO letters, NO logo. Serene layered mountain ridges and a winding river valley at dawn, muted deep indigo and violet in the foreground (#111021, #3B0D94), soft lavender haze (#ACA9FF), gentle amber sunrise (#FFD8A0) near the upper-right third. Sophisticated atmospheric digital matte painting with subtle natural texture, restrained detail, elegant and quiet rather than dramatic. Wide composition, approximately 3:1 panorama, intended to crop to an even wider 5:1 app banner. Keep left half darker, spacious and uncluttered so live white interface headings can sit there. The mountains and winding water should be visible and lovely primarily on the right. The horizon is in the upper half. No people, no religious symbols, no buildings, no books, no desk, no frames, no UI components. Professional editorial quality, calm and welcoming, visually coherent with a purple book-and-desk-lamp logo. Opaque background.

Montserrat and Inter are imported from `@fontsource-variable` with embedded Latin and Latin Extended WOFF2 files. Their full SIL Open Font License texts ship in `obsidian/THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt`. No remote font endpoint is used.
